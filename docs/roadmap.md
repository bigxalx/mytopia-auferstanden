# Roadmap and Milestones

Dates below reflect current planning assumptions and should be treated as committed checkpoints unless explicitly revised.

## Phase 1 Goal

Launch the app for audience participation before the festival window and keep it active through summer checkpoints.

## Timeline

0. February 13, 2026: CMS decision deadline (critical planning gate).
1. March 15, 2026: testing starts.
2. April 30, 2026: app submitted to App Store and Play Store (latest acceptable submission date).
3. May 20, 2026: app launch target.
4. May 27 to May 31, 2026: Wildwechsel festival with first checkpoint.
5. June to September 2026: additional city checkpoints.

## Phase 2 Goal

Deliver live interaction functionality for rehearsals and premiere.

## Timeline

1. August 13, 2026 at 18:00: rehearsal start.
2. August 24, 2026: technical setup on original stage.
3. September 10, 2026 at 18:00: full technical run.
4. September 16, 2026 at 18:00: dress rehearsal.
5. September 17, 2026 at 19:30: final rehearsal.
6. September 18, 2026 at 19:30: premiere.

## Internal Delivery Cadence (Recommended)

1. Sprint 0 (now to February 20): architecture decisions, repo setup, content pipeline decision, legal and store prerequisites.
2. Sprint 1 (February 20 to March 15): playable alpha for core loop and internal testing.
3. Sprint 2 (March 16 to April 30): stabilization, content integration, compliance, store submission.
4. Sprint 3 (May): launch operations and hotfix handling.
5. Phase 2 track (June to September): PoC, integration, stage hardening.

## Schedule Risk Notes

- April 30 submission is late-tight; internal target should be around April 20, 2026 to absorb rejection/review delays.
- Apple/Google account readiness is a hard dependency and should be monitored weekly until confirmed active.
